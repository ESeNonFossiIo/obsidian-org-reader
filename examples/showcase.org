A short tour of the Org syntax that Org Reader renders. Switch to the source with the
"Edit" button in the view header to compare.

* Text and links
** Emphasis
Text can be *bold*, /italic/, _underlined_, +struck through+, =verbatim= or ~code~.
Markers combine: *bold /and italic/*. A line break inside a paragraph is just a space,
but a double backslash \\
starts a new line.

** Links
- An external link: [[https://orgmode.org][the Org manual]], or a bare URL: https://orgmode.org.
- A link to another file: [[file:other.org][other notes]].
- A link to a heading in this file: [[*Tasks][jump to the tasks]].
- A footnote reference.[fn:1]
